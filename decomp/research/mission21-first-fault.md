# Mission 21 first fault

`scripts/check-native-mission21.py` verifies the first fault in script 40 against
the hash-locked executable, Mission 21 level/header, and language data. The probe
executes the native VM, height query, opcode-1199 dispatcher/consumer, trigger
initializer, and linked completion path. Allocation, template copying, first
processing, deletion, worship presentation, and shared-reference lookup are
supplied leaves; this is not a full native playthrough or audiovisual comparison.

## Authored lifecycle

Turn zero stores marker 17's signed terrain height, exactly `457`, in variable
13. For tribe 1, the 31-mask branch first samples at turn 127 and then every 32
turns. While sentinel trigger 46 at `(40,130)` still has one use, any sampled
marker-17 height other than `457` seals the fault. Both `456` and `458` qualify;
a change returned to `457` before sampling does not.

Trigger 46 links model 31 at `(36,128)`, the ordinary Flatten controller. A
manual Flatten height change removes the sentinel and sets presentation latch
variable 28; the next sample advances variable 22 to resolved state `2`. If the
sentinel was consumed directly, message 10 is posted and the same cleanup runs.

The level header makes Convert Wild and Flatten rechargeable but supplies no
stock or progress. Fresh native tribe initialization supplies `30000` mana;
Flatten costs `125000`, so the starting Shaman cannot charge it before the first
deadline. There is no Blue script grant or linked Flatten-stock reward. The
authored route is trigger 46 itself: its linked model-31 totem requires three
worshippers and 50 four-turn samples. Six Wild Braves begin beside the Shaman;
focusing the initial mana on the `10000`-cost Convert Wild spell recruits enough
followers to complete the totem before turn 1951. The browser acceptance drives
that charging, HUD cast, conversion, and worship path without injecting stock or
people. Full native cursor, pathfinding, and route timing remain unrecorded.

If height remains `457`, the strict deadline predicate is `turn > 1950`; the
first eligible turn is 1951. Marker 17 forces trigger 68, whose ordered links are
model 15 (the ordinary Volcano controller) and model 39. Variable 22 advances to
dispatched state `1`, sentinel 46 is removed, and positioned message 126 is
posted at `(34,126)` with payload `16018`, head flag `0x200`, and lifetime 256.
At turn 1983, state `1` advances to resolved state `2` and cleanup runs.

Variables 19, 22, and 28 prevent duplicate initial activation, eruption, and
success presentation. Checkpoints must preserve them alongside baseline variable
13, the sampled terrain, sentinel/trigger consumption, active Flatten or Volcano,
and the queued message. The browser's structured world clone owns that state.

## Opcode 1199

`00490ba0` resolves `(x,y,radius)` and calls `004f1ed0`. The first fault passes
`(36,126,5)`, scanning the inclusive coarse-cell square `x=26..46`,
`y=116..136` in steps of two. It deletes only class-7 models 66, 75, 79, and 85;
models 15, 39, and 31 survive. Opcode 1199 is visual cleanup after the script's
seal/eruption decision, not the cancellation owner.

Initial component triggers are forced once in marker order 91, 92, 93 and clone
their linked records in authored slot order: nine model-79 records, five model-75
records, and ten model-85 records. Exact model-39 and component presentation,
later faults, Mission 21 AI, and mission outcome remain open.
