Canonical executable SHA-256 is `3a5065c7420b3fcde208bf220bc86dfbac95e025ab2492caf9c7ea5308dfbe4f`; GNU objdump 2.44 decoded bounded original bytes without executing original instructions. The [independent review](../../references/verification/swarm-building-static-2026-10-09/static-review.md) accepts the controller interpretation, not native/browser equivalence.

## Recovered contract

`00510120` searches spiral indices 0..166 around the saved cast origin when remaining lifetime is divisible by 64, then chooses the first class-2 object of another tribe absent from ten remembered IDs. Native cell-linked order owns the selection. Occupancy, building model, completion and diplomacy are not acquisition predicates. A full history consumes one RNG draw modulo ten; free-slot insertion consumes none.

Ten separate controller substages govern outside approach, insect entry, six physical-slot ejections, dwell and insect exit. Ejection removes occupancy before clearing forced-exit movement, optionally initializes panic, applies removal/damage and finally reveals Spies. Its person filters/order differ from the ground scan. Child arrival, movement/RNG, target-loss handling and lifetime continue through their recovered owners. Full instruction-address references and source bindings are in the [accepted findings](../../references/verification/swarm-building-static-2026-10-09/findings.md).

## Live integration boundary

Current main at `d6cf109474379172728a3ccebf46ef72a15f3a58` has no Swarm building phase. The [source correspondence report](../../references/verification/swarm-building-static-2026-10-09/integration-followup.md) identifies the missing native class-2 cell registration owner: existing `object-cells.ts` mechanics are generic, but their live registration/cleanup belongs to people; building footprint handles do not preserve object-list order. An array or nearest-building selector would not be equivalent.

Occupancy removal should use its existing physical-slot owner and the exact returned person. The recovered transition preserves command queues, requiring explicit preserve-mode panic for unprotected occupants and preservation of protected-person ownership. The [independent correspondence review](../../references/verification/swarm-building-static-2026-10-09/integration-review.md) accepts these source conclusions; no runtime change is delivered here.

Mission 3 already provides player Swarm and recurring Chumara settlement/training, but no initial Chumara building. Tower completion is not proof of occupation, and Temple occupancy can be transient. An ordinary eligible occupied-building episode still needs demonstration after the real membership/order mapping exists. The packet grants no broader AI, audio, checkpoint, lifetime/browser or full-campaign acceptance; #61 remains open.
