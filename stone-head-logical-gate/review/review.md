## What the evidence proves

Its complete JSON equals the retained result after excluding only elapsed seconds.

source and raw streams. Two creation cases and four 66-row timelines passed.

initializers, rather than supplying the gate. Instruction 004a5fdc adds 0x40000;
004ed580 subsequently adds bit4 and stamps the current outer counter. The created
record is class5/model9/state10, flags3 0x40004 from zero input. A seeded 0x100 is
preserved without being required for the gate.

The real three-object post-load linker derives the automatic-gift flag from the
linked class6/model2 record. Actual selector/setters select object45/draw4/morph1,
presentation mode2, with the gate retained and no transition bit0x1000. The

The real class5 state10 processor 004a6480→004a8b00 executes before 004ed700 writes
the stamp. It is not replaced by a no-op. The actual 004ee770 primary/secondary
animation traversal, hold check and 004ee7b0 updater then execute. Ordinary mode4
advances only when stamped, giving frames 1,1,2,2,… on the supplied alternating

visit pattern. There are 61 raw-f1 and 54 visible-frame differences per native
timeline. Deliberately clearing only the gate makes every compared f1/frame/hold-

Disabled rows hold displayed frame1 while retaining raw f1. Refill runs actual

transition, nor cover the entire transition lifecycle.

This is stronger than the earlier geometry probe's supplied zero-flag scenery:

point/morph/per-eligible-visit geometry evidence is not invalidated or relabelled

## Limits preserved

The original bank2 object, face, point and morph bytes are hash-verified and the
normalizer executes. Read-only PE sections are guarded unchanged. Terrain height,
insertion/invalidation, shadow, neighbor notifications, propagation and audio
submission are supplied leaves. The probe uses an intact state10 head in that
controlled environment; it does not establish all terrain-damage or neighbor
effects. The sampled class processor itself, frame/hold logic and family selection
are original instructions.

The allocation lists, counters and visit schedule are fixtures. Full allocation
and the full logical outer traversal are not executed. The secondary list is a
traversal control, not proof that authored heads normally reside there. The first
logical row follows creation, so the packet does not dynamically test a new
allocation's first draw without an intervening processor. The wrapper stamp is
direct evidence for that ownership boundary; a blanket fresh-allocation timing
claim would exceed this probe. No original OS, wall-clock frequency, native GPU,
full audio/cue193 or complete campaign execution is claimed.

The supported owner is the decorative class5/model9 body selected into the existing
authored model45/46/47 family. Its linked class6 trigger/gift is a different object.
Vault bodies, knowledge/world/UI HFX, other Stone Head models, model149/static157,
model8 sequences and generic scenery are excluded. There is no basis for changing
global24Hz or sharing timing across those families.

## Minimal implementation boundary

1. Restore the gate only for the existing source-validated StoneHeadAnimation
   family45 path and bind its ordinary mode4 work to each completed logical turn,
   using the existing chronological owner. Adding the flag alone leaves
   stamp0==counter0 and does not fix extra visits. Reuse the branch-aware gate
   distinction so ungated transition work cannot advance in both phases.
2. Keep controller notifications separate from animation advancement.
   syncStoneHeadPresentation/syncStoneHeadEnabled must continue to record every
   disable/refill transition without consuming a visit. Preserve the current
   observer order before logical animation and before coincident presentation;
   do not batch all turn-dependent transitions into one final rendered state.
3. The body outlives its trigger. Current stepWorshipHead visits non-Vault heads
   before the inactive-trigger early return, and rendering keeps a recognized
   decorative state visible independently. Do not use shrine.active, remaining
   rewards, follower count or trigger retirement as the animation-owner filter.
   Disabled means held frame1; exhausted trigger does not imply deleted body.
4. Initialization/migration must preserve saved f1/f2, hold mode and authored
   identity while repairing the missing gate. Existing world creation initializes
   the family; renderer/clock paths can also lazily initialize old optional state.
   Those pure reads or lazy initialization must not grant extra advancement.
   Missing old state may retain the established phase0/held1 reconstruction policy
   without inventing historical phase or replaying rewards. Old stamp0 and a new
   Scene clock must not replay a completed visit.
5. Preserve separate heads, reset-on-refill, same-interval disable/refill, pause,
   land pause, speed0 and slow/fast/catch-up behavior. Use the recognized decorative
   body's allocation/visit boundary; if a new runtime head producer is added,
   make its first-visit policy explicit rather than generalizing the supplied-list
   fixture to full allocator behavior. Keep raw geometry, cached coordinates,
   imported faces/UVs, cue ownership, rewards and both RNG streams unchanged.

Existing tests and the old focused browser helper contain presentation-only
assumptions. In particular, that browser helper sets speed0 and advances 1/24 to
sample successive body phases. Preserve its per-phase geometry assertions while
adapting the elapsed-gameplay portion to the real logical owner or clearly
separating a controlled geometry fixture. Do not retain speed0 body advancement
or weaken phase assertions merely to keep that helper passing. The exhausted-head
half-interval test likewise needs to distinguish an extra presentation hold from
the next real logical visit.

Before candidate acceptance, use focused failure-first constructor/legacy-save,
phase separation, disabled/refill, retired-trigger, multi-head and catch-up tests;
compare the actual candidate adapter against these retained native rows; then run
standard/affected gates and a source-bound ordinary visible model45 witness with
raw phase, logical turn, presentation serial and rendered geometry. Keep optional
geometry fixtures and unsampled families labelled. No additional full native
renderer/lifecycle probe is required merely to begin this bounded fix.
