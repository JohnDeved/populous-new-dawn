## Archive format and limits

`manifest.json` pins each origin, exact expanded bytes/hash and stored bytes/hash. Gzip archives use a deterministic timestamp. PNGs and reviews retain their original bytes. Browser temporary caches/profiles are omitted; execution receipts, raw outputs, per-turn/RAF rows, source/native/static checks, pixels and reviews are retained.

These results establish the bounded ranged-phase fields, append-only artwork preservation, ordinary building-target capture/recovery, and actual submitted texture path. They do not establish absolute operating-system/hardware timing, full original-game scene parity, whole-command equivalence, facing parity or post-completion field ownership.
