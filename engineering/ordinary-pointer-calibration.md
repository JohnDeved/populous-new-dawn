## Timing quantities and derived envelope algorithm

For event i, retain separately:
1. Input-listener observation→observed completion = observedCompletedAt_i − evidence.timestampMs_i. The latter is host.performance.now() sampled inside the capture listener, NOT the PointerEvent.timeStamp. This quantity includes subsequent work/callback phase, excludes pre-listener delivery delay, and is NOT pure queue latency. Actual parent event.timeStamp is retained in packet.events column3; normalized underlying sample timestamp is column4; listener observation is column5. RAF timestamp is separate, not completion time. Preserve all four clocks separately; do not substitute one for another.
2. Previous selected completion→input observation = evidence.timestampMs_i − observedCompletedAt_(i−1). Includes Node/browser roundtrips, control operations and scheduling; candidate/runtime speed can influence it.
3. Last observed idle callback completion→input observation, all intervening serial counts and RAF/completion intervals. These reveal pacing; they are not independent samples or pure external confound measurements.

Use p50=ordinary median and p95=nearest rank ceil(0.95*n)−1. Session summaries cover all48 measured events (the first uses warmup event6 as predecessor), with separate per-cycle and first-cycle-versus-cycles2–8 descriptors. Preserve absolute timestamps, no equality-matching of timestamps between sessions.

Before running C1, freeze this recipe for an engineering scheduling envelope. For each session compute p50 and p95 of quantities1 and2, and median/p95 serial differences between successive selections. For each metric m across three valid calibration sessions, let L=min(C_m), U=max(C_m), M=median(C_m). Set D=max(F_m,0.25*M), and envelope [max(0,L−D),U+D]. Fixed floors: quantity1 p50=2ms,p95=5ms; quantity2 p50=50ms,p95=100ms; serial p50/p95=1 callback. These floors are explicit practical tolerance choices, not claimed statistical estimates. Do not widen them after any failed calibration, holdout or candidate result.

Calibration stability prerequisite: U−L must be ≤2D for every metric; otherwise report scheduling calibration INCONCLUSIVE and stop. Freeze actual numeric envelope, calibration source hashes and all three raw artifacts BEFORE reading/launching held-out A/A sessions. A missing calibration result is not a successful partial envelope.

A/A scheduling feasibility requires every held-out session metric inside the frozen envelope and exact per-event work/witness equality. Any outside-envelope session is timing-INCONCLUSIVE, not invalid raw data and not a reason to delete or rerun it. Continue all six predeclared held-out sessions after a timing-only envelope failure, within the unchanged aggregate resource budget; retain the failed qualification state. A hard protocol-validity, resource or cleanup failure instead aborts subsequent launches and marks the cohort incomplete. No replacement or rerun in either case. Compare within-session first-cycle versus remaining-cycle descriptors and publish them; no post-hoc warmup edits. Their observed drift is part of the qualified workload, not evidence of steady state.

## Same-source timing controls and later candidate boundary

Compute existing primary ordinary triangle-scan per-query frame-average p50/p95 and secondary total-terrain-pick p50/p95 per SESSION, using the exact established numeric units/aggregation after independently confirming the collector-to-metric binding. Keep raw per-frame work and duration; division by zero fails validity. All48 selected frames contribute. Do not pretend frame-average per-query p95 equals individual query-tail latency.

For each metric report all three pair differences and the existing conservative descriptive floor max(0.1ms,5%baseline session-median,2×baseline range), repeat spread max(0.1ms,15%median). A/A must produce no directional possible-improvement/regression signal under the unchanged rule that all three differences clear the floor in one direction. A directional A/A signal or excessive repeat spread means INCONCLUSIVE and stops candidate admission. Zero signal is only feasibility evidence from three pairs, not proof of negligible bias or guaranteed sensitivity. No p-values from48 samples, no tuning floor downward, no automatic successful label from non-significance.

Only after independent review of complete calibration+A/A may a separately authorized B/H protocol be frozen. Completion delay and inter-event gaps are endogenous to measured work: a later candidate slowdown cannot be invalidated or hidden merely because these increase. Keep all raw data, report the CPU outcome and mark cross-session scheduling interpretation INCONCLUSIVE where outside-envelope; never filter slow candidate frames or substitute sessions to manufacture comparability. A candidate faster than the envelope also is not automatically comparable. A/A does not grant unlimited future source/runtime admission; freeze exact B/H/harness identities and require the same work/output proof. Existing#887 thresholds remain unchanged. Causal speedup or allocation claims require further support; this design itself grants none.

## Implemented route and resource containment

A separate trusted-main workflow_dispatch verifies refs/heads/main plus exact expected SHA; PR code cannot run qualification as its own base. One production Vite fixture build is reused by nine fresh sequential supported Chrome processes, with unchanged sandbox/args. Existing functional smoke and all default matrices remain unchanged. Each session retains the90s outer smoke bound including preparation/capture,30s browser launch cap and2s owner-close limits. A monotonic1200s aggregate timer stops further launches and attempts bounded known-owner cleanup; shell1500s TERM+5sKILL and30min hosted job provide outer containment. These are maximum bounds, not a guarantee every slow run can complete. Late/uncertain operations remain failures, not retroactive success.

Artifact limits are8MiB/file,32MiB/session,8MiB combined aggregate manifests/outerreceipt/buildlog,296MiBoverall. Whole packet receipts and raw numeric evidence remain retained; no truncation or selective deduplication. A quota violation is hard failure, not permission to discard unfavorable input. The three calibration raw hashes and envelope are written once before heldouts, then rechecked by SHA256 before each heldout. Exact binary identity is checked in1MiB chunks before and after every session. Runtime reader/source/bundle dependencies, geometry and every ordered work/result tuple must agree.

The implementation may be executed only from independently reviewed/tested merged trusted main through this explicit route, under standing performance authorization. No additional user approval is required. Independent correctness review and resource/admission checks remain mandatory. A/A success grants feasibility only; candidate comparison must retain the separately frozen exactsource/harness/protocol and normal review gates. No automated benchmark default, comparison or source-profile expansion is introduced.

Stopping conditions: hard validity/resource/cleanup failure aborts subsequent launches and marks incomplete. Calibration instability after all three valid calibration sessions prevents heldout launches. Timing-only heldout failures latch INCONCLUSIVE but retain all six predeclared sessions under the same aggregate bound. Directional A/A signal or excessive repeat spread makes final qualification INCONCLUSIVE. Never replace failed sessions or tune thresholds after data.
