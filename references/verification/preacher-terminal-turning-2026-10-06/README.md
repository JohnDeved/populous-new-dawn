## Limits

The observed ordinary Preacher projection supplies the initial assignment/flags and
RNG words. Counter alignment, five already-owned listeners, topology and relocated
records are deliberate component supplies. No ordinary five-listener history,
original allocator/scheduler/OS execution, victim updates, rendered impact or
whole-sermon parity is established. Modes 1/2 continuation, ghost/Bloodlust and
command 32 expiry remain separate boundaries. An actual production port-caller
failure and reviewed correction are required before a later runtime patch or merge.
