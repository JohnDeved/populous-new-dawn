# Bounded eight-Brave Temple admission comparison

## Result and evidence scope

The original CPU composition reaches five occupants and three waiting Braves after 48 turns. Actual `0x407150` calls succeed once each on turns **33, 40, 43, 46, 48**, for IDs **1832, 1833, 1834, 1835, 1837**. `entering` reaches five including admitted residents; maximum outside substate-5 count is four. The remaining queue is **1839 → 1836 → 1838**.

This is an isolated, flat-land, source-bound composition result. The omitted-phase audit first found 49 queued terrain updates at `0x6906d8`. Controlled final attempt **08** resolves this dependency by executing complete original `0x44df40` at its late native scheduler phase: all 49 cells are processed and the queue clears on turn 1. Those updates materially change terrain flags/categories, but the complete person/formation/route/command/admission trajectory is exactly equal to attempts 06/07. This establishes outcome irrelevance for the tested fixture by execution, rather than by dismissing the missing phase. It remains a bounded composition, not full-original-game startup or scheduling.

The trace does not establish a hard one-person semaphore. Five total residents are admitted, and this fixture has staggered successful admissions. It does not prove framebuffer appearance, animation cadence, ordinary UI click processing, training completion, or departure timing.

Final behavioral attempt **08**, following 06 and its observation-only repeat 07, executes:

4. Complete building `0x403280`; conversion branch is naturally disabled by fixture game flag 32.

**Zero function bodies were replaced or stubbed.** All hooks either observe or abort unsupported memory/interrupt/out-of-image execution. There are no canned return values. Original logical animation-list stepping `0x4ee770`, full frame/outer loop and rendering are excluded.

The successful trace records 384 person visits, 384 real motion calls, 404 route advances, 344 collision calls, 384 command-8 visits, 244 formation-recruitment attempts, seven formation controller calls, five admissions and five original indicator updates. This report makes no claim that a rendered full game would preserve the same trajectory or timing.
