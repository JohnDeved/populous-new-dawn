## What this pass resolves

The loader has 52 direct person records in a definite file order, but its first record is a completed Blue Hut whose nested initializer runs before any of those people. Direct building initialization is not itself a proved person allocator. Its retained state-2 initializer has no applicable person allocation, but a conditional class-6/model-9 child introduces a still-unclosed constructor. The unresolved seed cannot be replaced by 52, by selected-person order, or by the browser's world-turn counter. Existing inputs are present. Remaining work is source/callback closure and state/order proof, not a missing-file or permission blocker.
